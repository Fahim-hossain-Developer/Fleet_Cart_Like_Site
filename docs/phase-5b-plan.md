## Safety and preservation
No private users/orders/credentials/marketing membership are demo input/export. Merge never overwrites owner records. Replace archives affected content after explicit typed confirmation; products referenced by orders are hidden/soft-deleted. Snapshot restoration must not rewind orders, payments, identity or unrelated edits made after import. Provenance includes original/current fingerprints so cleanup/rollback detects owner edits and concurrent operations. No arbitrary SQL/model class/template/script from a pack; bounded typed references and media allowlists. No ZIP path traversal/symlinks/remote assets/executable uploads. No external messages or actual provider charge is sent from development.

Authoritative money stays integer; landing package prices and upsell offers are server records, not browser totals. Preserve original receipts and payment-environment taint, forbid financial side effects from demo operations, and never use marketing verification to establish account ownership. No fake purchase/social-proof tracking from sample reviews or importer activity.
